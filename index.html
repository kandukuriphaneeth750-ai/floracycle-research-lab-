MEASUREMENT HELPERS

Useful formulas

Use consistent units and record raw measurements as well as calculated values.

WATER ABSORPTION((wet mass − dry mass) ÷ dry mass) × 100

Report soak time and temperature too.

MASS LOSS((initial mass − final mass) ÷ initial mass) × 100

Specify test conditions and blank/control.

ESTIMATED COSTtotal material cost ÷ usable bags

Include waste and rejected samples.

START WITH TRUSTED EVIDENCE

Sources & reading list

Starter literature and official guidance relevant to starch-based films, agricultural residues, and biodegradable-plastic claims.

PEER-REVIEWED · 2022

The effect of rice straw derived microfibrillated cellulose as a reinforcing agent in starch/PVA/PEG biocompatible films

Research on rice-straw-derived cellulose as a reinforcing component in polymer films. Read the methods closely before deciding whether its ingredients are suitable for your low-cost project.

Open publisher page ↗
PEER-REVIEWED · 2023

Antioxidant starch composite films containing rice straw extract and cellulose fibres

Studies rice straw extract and cellulose fibres in thermoplastic starch films, including mechanical and barrier properties.

Open publisher page ↗
OPEN ACCESS · 2025

Biodegradable Film of Starch-Based Carboxymethyl Cellulose from Rice Husk and Straw for Application in Food Preservation

Investigates rice husk and straw cellulose conversion and starch-based CMC films. Useful for comparing processing complexity and material properties.

Read full article ↗
OPEN ACCESS · 2023

Development and characterization of starch-based bioactive thermoplastic packaging films derived from banana peels

Uses banana-peel-derived starch and cellulose in films; a useful alternative agricultural-waste comparison, but not proof that a rice-straw carry bag will perform the same way.

Read article ↗
